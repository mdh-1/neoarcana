About the deck

Fair questions, plainly answered.

How are the cards drawn?

With true randomness. Each shuffle requests atmospheric-noise random numbers from random.org — the same source used for lotteries and scientific draws. If that service is unavailable, we fall back to your operating system's cryptographic randomness, and the reading says so.

Who writes the interpretation?

A language model, given your cards, their positions and your question. The card meanings shown beneath each plate are traditional Rider–Waite and Thoth attributions, held in our own deck data — not generated.

Can a reversed card appear?

Yes — every card, including The Fool, has an equal and independent chance of arriving reversed. (An earlier version of this site could never reverse The Fool. We fixed the arithmetic.)

Is this fortune-telling?

No. A spread is a mirror, not a window: it offers language and structure for thinking about your situation. It is offered for reflection, not prediction, and it is no substitute for professional advice.

What happens to my question?

It is sent to the interpreting model along with the drawn cards, kept briefly in memory so your reading's page keeps working, and not written to any database.
